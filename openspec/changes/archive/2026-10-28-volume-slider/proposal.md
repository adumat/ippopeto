# Proposal

## Why

The game is loud: it starts at full level, and the only control is mute. Players want to keep the sound on, just lower.

## What Changes

- The speaker button opens a small volume panel with a slider (0–100 %) and the mute toggle.
- The volume starts at 40 % and each browser remembers its own choice. 100 % is today's level.
- `M` still toggles mute.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `product/look-and-sound`: the game sounds get a volume level besides mute.
