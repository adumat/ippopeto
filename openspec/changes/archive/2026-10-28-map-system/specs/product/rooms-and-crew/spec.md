# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Lobby with free practice

After boarding, a seated player SHALL see the lobby: the current map with its name and thumbnail and the map selector, the crew list with colours and free seats, "Copia il link", "Si parte", and a short key guide. The vehicle SHALL already be on the current map and fully controllable, so the crew can practise. Bites during practice SHALL NOT be counted.

#### Scenario: Practising before the start
- **WHEN** the crew pushes in the lobby
- **THEN** the vehicle moves on the current map, and no timer runs
